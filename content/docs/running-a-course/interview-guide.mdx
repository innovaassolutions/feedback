---
title: The interview guide
description: The rating questions and conversation topics the interviewer covers, for every session of the course.
---

Under **Admin**, open **Interview guide**. The guide applies to every session of the course. The interviewer asks the ratings first, then the topics.

## Rating items

Each rating item has a **Question** and its **Choices**, one per line. **Prefer not to say** is always added, so don't add it yourself.

Concrete choices work better than agree/disagree scales. "Yes, with the guide beside me" tells you what to do next, and "Agree" doesn't. The defaults:

| Item | Question | Choices |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ability | Could you now do what this session taught, on your own? | Yes, without help · Yes, with the guide beside me · Only with someone helping · Not yet |
| Pace | How was the pace of the session? | Too slow · About right · Too fast |
| Relevance | How much of this session will you use in your work? | Most of it · Some of it · A little · None yet |
| Recommend | How likely are you to recommend this session to a colleague, from 0 to 10? | 0 to 10 |

The dashboard's tiles read the ability, pace and recommend items, so change their wording rather than what they measure.

## Conversation topics

The interviewer covers the topics in order, with one question per turn and at most one follow-up per topic. Each topic has:

- **Label**: a short name.
- **What to find out**: the goal. The interviewer words the actual question itself.
- **Opening question**: a plain question for when the platform, not the AI, has to ask.

Untick **Include** to leave a topic out. The defaults are: Most useful, Unclear, Blockers, Change next time, From the session brief, and Last session's changes. The interviewer asks "From the session brief" only when the session has an approved brief, and "Last session's changes" only when changes were recorded.

Choose **Save guide**. Each save creates a new version, shown at the top of the page. Change the guide between sessions rather than while interviews are under way.

<Callout title="The guide is not the interviewer's rulebook">
  The guide sets what to ask. The rules, such as one question at a time, no leading questions, the turn limit and accepting "Prefer not to say", are built into the platform and apply whatever the guide says.
</Callout>
