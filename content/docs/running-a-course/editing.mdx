---
title: Renaming and correcting
description: Everything you create can be renamed or corrected. Courses and sessions can be deleted until they hold learner feedback.
---

Anything set up by mistake can be put right: a course named like a session, a session out of order, a learner's name misspelt.

| What | Where | What you can change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Course** | **Course setup → Course details**, or **Rename or delete** under **Settings → Courses** | Its title, web address and settings, or delete it |
| **Session** | Open the session, then **Edit details** | Its number, title, date and objectives, or **Delete session** |
| **Document** | **Edit** under the document | Its title and who can see it, or **Remove** it |
| **Learner** | **Course setup → Learners**, then **Edit name** | Their name, or **Remove** them from the course |
| **Instructor, sponsor or admin** | **People**, then **Edit name** | Their name in every role, or **Remove** a role |
| **Product documentation** | **Course setup → Sessions**, under **Product documentation** | **Read again** now, or **Remove** the site and its pages |
| **Organization** | **Branding**, then **Rename or change the web address** | Its name and web address |

## Deleting

A course or session can be deleted only while it has no learner feedback, and a session only while it has no questions either. Feedback is never deleted by accident: once learners have given it, rename the course or session instead. Deleting removes everything set up under it: a course's sessions, documents and roster, or a session's documents.

Removing a learner from a course keeps their account and any feedback they gave.

## Web addresses

An organization's or a course's web address can be changed, but every link, QR code and email already sent uses the old one and stops working. Change it before you share the course. Only an organization admin can change one.

## Session numbers

Sessions are numbered in the order they're added. To change the order, change a session's number under **Edit details**; two sessions can't share a number.
