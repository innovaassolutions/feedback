Instructors manage sessions under **Admin** in the course header. The **Sessions** tab lists every session with its state.

## Creating a session

Under **New session**, enter a **Title**, the **Date** and the **Objectives**: what learners should be able to do afterwards. Choose **Create draft session**. The session starts as a draft, which learners can't see.

Then open the session to add its documents and approve its brief. See [Documents and the session brief](/docs/running-a-course/documents-and-brief).

## Session states

| State | On screen | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Draft | **Draft** | Being set up. Hidden from learners |
| Scheduled | **Upcoming** | Visible to learners, not delivered yet |
| Open | **Feedback open** | Delivered. Feedback and Q&A are open |
| Closed | **Closed** | The feedback window has ended. Q&A stays open |

A session moves forward one step at a time with **Move to …**: Draft → Upcoming → Feedback open → Closed. It can't move back. This keeps the record straight: feedback given on an open session can't end up on a session that was never delivered.

When you move a session to **Feedback open**, its feedback window starts. It closes by itself after a week (the course's feedback window), and you can close it sooner yourself.

## Reminders

Learners who haven't given feedback get two emails: one a day after the session opens, and one a day before it closes. Learners who have submitted get none.

## The join link and QR code

Each session's admin page shows its **Join link** and a QR code. Put the QR code on your last slide: learners scan it in the room and go straight to the feedback interview, signing in first if they need to. Feedback collected in the room gets far more responses than a link sent later.

## A good rhythm

1. Before the session: create it, upload the materials, approve the brief, move it to **Upcoming**.
2. At the end of the session: move it to **Feedback open** and show the QR code.
3. Before the next session: read the results, record what you changed in **You said, we did**.
