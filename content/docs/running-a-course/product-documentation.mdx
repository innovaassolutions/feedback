---
title: Product documentation
description: Connect the organization's documentation site so the Q&A can answer from it.
---

If your course teaches a product with its own documentation site, connect it. The librarian then answers learners' questions from it as well as from the session documents.

Under **Course setup**, at the foot of the **Sessions** tab, under **Product documentation**, enter the **Documentation site**, such as `https://docs.example.com/`, and choose **Add documentation**. This needs an organization admin.

- Only pages **under that address** are read. Links to other sites aren't followed.
- The site's rules for robots are respected, and reading stops at 500 pages.
- The site is **re-read every week**, so changed pages are updated and removed pages are dropped.
- Documentation belongs to the **organization**, so every course it runs can use it.

Answers that use it cite the page, marked **Documentation**, with a link.
