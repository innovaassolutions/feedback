---
title: Branding
description: Upload a logo, and the AI brand designer proposes a theme. Nothing changes until you approve it.
---

Organization admins open **Settings → Branding**.

## 1. Propose a theme

Upload the **Logo file**, a PNG or SVG up to 2 MB, and choose **Propose a theme from this logo**. The brand designer reads the logo and proposes colors for light and dark mode, a corner radius, and a heading and body font from an approved list.

## 2. Check the preview

The preview shows the theme on real parts of the platform, in **Light mode** and dark mode side by side: the header, a dashboard tile, a chart and the interview chat.

## 3. Adjust

Under **Adjust**, change any color, the **Corner radius**, the **Heading font** or the **Body font**. The preview updates as you go.

## 4. Approve

Choose **Approve and make live**. Every page in the organization uses the theme from the next page load: course pages, the dashboard and its charts, the interview, the sign-in page and the printed report. The logo appears in the header, on the sign-in page and on the printed report.

## In emails

Every email the platform sends for your organization uses its live theme: sign-in links and invitations, feedback reminders, the follow-up invitation, blocker alerts and questions sent to instructors. Each has your logo on a band of your primary color, your fonts where the reader's device has them, and a button in your primary color. Mail apps that follow dark mode show the theme's dark colors.

Gmail and Outlook don't show SVG images, so emails carry your logo as a PNG made from the file you uploaded. A new logo or theme applies to emails from the moment it's live.

Someone in more than one organization gets a sign-in link in the look of the organization whose page they signed in from. An email never shows an organization the person isn't part of.

<Callout title="Readable by design">
  Every text and background pair must have a contrast ratio of at least 4.5 to 1, the accessibility standard for normal text. The brand designer's proposal is adjusted until it passes, and a theme that fails can't be approved.
</Callout>

## Theme history

**Theme history** lists earlier themes. Choose **Restore** to make one live again.

Light and dark mode follow each person's system setting.
