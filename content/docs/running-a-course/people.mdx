---
title: People
description: Add organization admins, instructors and sponsors. Each gets an invitation by email.
---

Organization admins open **Settings → People**, or **People** on the organization's page. Platform admins can open any organization's People page from **Platform administration**.

Learners aren't added here: each course's [Roster](/docs/running-a-course/roster) adds them.

## Adding a person

Under **Add a person**, enter their **Name** and **Email address**, choose a **Role**, and for an instructor or sponsor choose the **Course**. Choose **Add and email a sign-in link**.

| Role | What it gives |
| --- | --- |
| **Organization admin** | Manages branding, courses and people, sets up every course, and sees every course's results and transcripts as its instructors do. Answering questions stays with instructors. |
| **Instructor** | Runs one course: sessions, documents, the roster and the interview guide. Sees its results and transcripts, and answers learners' questions. |
| **Sponsor** | Views one course's outcomes: the dashboard, themes, **You said, we did** and the printed report. Never transcripts, quotes or anything from a single learner. For executives and managers. See [For sponsors](/docs/instructors/sponsors). |

The person gets an account if they have none, and an email in your organization's branding. Someone who has never signed in gets an invitation that says what they've been given and how signing in works; someone who has gets a sign-in link.

To give someone a role on two courses, add them once for each course. A person can be both an instructor and a sponsor, or a learner on one course and an instructor on another.

<Callout title="One email a minute">
  Only one sign-in email goes to an address each minute. If you give someone two roles within a minute, the second is saved but its email isn't sent; they can ask for a link on the sign-in page.
</Callout>

Each person shows whether they've signed in yet. **Resend invitation** (or **Send a sign-in email** once they have) emails them a new link and code.

## Removing a role

Choose **Remove** next to the person, then **Yes, remove**. Only that role goes: their account and any other roles stay. You can't remove your own organization admin role; another admin can.

## Platform admins

Platform admins create organizations and can manage every organization, its courses and its people. On **Platform administration**, the **Platform admins** list shows them all and adds new ones the same way. Nobody can remove their own platform admin access.
