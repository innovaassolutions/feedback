---
title: Courses
description: Organization admins create courses. Each has its own sessions, roster and guide.
---

An organization admin opens the organization's page (its address, such as `/streamspace`) and chooses **Manage courses**, or goes to **Settings → Courses**.

Under **New course**, enter the **Course title** and the **Course address**, the short name in its web address such as `ime-engineering`. Choose **Create course**. The course starts with the default interview guide and the standard [settings and limits](/docs/how-it-works/settings-and-limits).

Then:

1. [Invite learners](/docs/running-a-course/roster).
2. Have the platform team add the instructors and any sponsor.
3. [Create the first session](/docs/running-a-course/sessions).

Each course's pages live under the organization's address, such as `/streamspace/ime-engineering`.
