---
title: Courses
description: Organization admins create courses. Each has its own sessions, roster and guide.
---

An organization admin opens the organization's page (its address, such as `/streamspace`) and chooses **Manage courses**, or goes to **Settings → Courses**.

Under **New course**, enter the **Course title**. The **Web address** fills in from the title; it's the short name in the course's web address, such as `ime-engineering` for `/streamspace/ime-engineering`, and can't be changed later. Choose **Create course**. The course starts with the default interview guide and the standard [settings and limits](/docs/how-it-works/settings-and-limits).

Then:

1. [Invite learners](/docs/running-a-course/roster).
2. Add the instructors and any sponsors under **Settings → People**. See [People](/docs/running-a-course/people).
3. [Create the first session](/docs/running-a-course/sessions).

Each course's pages live under the organization's address, such as `/streamspace/ime-engineering`.
