A course is a series of sessions: one for each class, workshop or module. Its learners, instructors, sponsors and interview guide apply to all of them; each session has its own documents, brief, feedback and Q&A.

An organization admin opens the organization's page (its address, such as `/streamspace`) and chooses **Manage courses**, or goes to **Settings → Courses**.

Under **New course**, enter the **Course title**. The **Web address** fills in from the title; it's the short name in the course's web address, such as `ime-engineering` for `/streamspace/ime-engineering`, and can't be changed later. Choose **Create course**. Its **Course setup** opens, ready for its sessions. The course starts with the default interview guide and the standard [settings and limits](/docs/how-it-works/settings-and-limits).

Then, in its **Course setup**:

1. [Add its sessions](/docs/running-a-course/sessions), in order, and set each one up with its documents and brief.
2. [Invite its learners](/docs/running-a-course/roster) on the **Learners** tab.
3. Add its instructors and any sponsors under **People**. See [People](/docs/running-a-course/people).

**Settings → Courses** lists every course with its number of sessions and learners.

Each course's pages live under the organization's address, such as `/streamspace/ime-engineering`.
