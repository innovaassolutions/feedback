---
title: Documents and the session brief
description: Upload a session's materials. The AI librarian reads them, drafts the brief, and answers questions from them.
---

Under **Course setup**, open a session from the **Sessions** list. Its **Documents** and **Session brief** are steps 1 and 2 of its page.

## Uploading documents

Choose a **File**, give it a **Title** (it defaults to the file name), choose **Who can see it**, then choose **Upload document**.

- **Accepted files:** PDF, Word (DOCX), Markdown, plain text, and transcripts of recorded sessions as VTT or SRT files (or as Word or text files), up to 25 MB.
- **Learners and agents:** learners can open and download it from the session page, and the AI uses it.
- **Agents only:** only the AI uses it, to draft the brief and answer questions. It's never listed or linked for learners or sponsors. Use this for trainer notes or a session recording's transcript.

Files go straight from your browser to the platform's private storage, then are checked: a file whose contents don't match its type, such as a renamed file, is deleted and refused.

<Callout title="Upload the recording's transcript">
  The librarian answers learners' questions about what a session covered from its documents, and the transcript is the fullest record of that. Most recording tools export one: Microsoft Teams and Zoom as VTT, others as SRT, Word or text. Upload it as **Agents only**. Where the transcript and the product documentation differ, for example a button renamed since the recording, the librarian gives the documentation's current version, says what the session showed, and you're told.
</Callout>

After uploading, the document shows **Reading…** while the librarian extracts the text, then **Ready**. If a file can't be read, for example a scanned PDF with no text or a corrupt file, it shows **Couldn't read** with the reason. Choose **Remove** to delete a document and everything taken from it.

## The session brief

Once the documents are read, the librarian drafts the session brief:

- **Objectives**: what learners should be able to do.
- **Topics covered**: the main topics, one per line.
- **Known issues**: anything learners are likely to struggle with.

Edit it, then choose **Approve brief**. If no draft appears a minute after the documents are read, or you've added documents since, choose **Draft the brief from the documents**. The interviewer uses only an approved brief. Until you approve it, interviews still work but don't ask about this session's particular topics. With an approved brief, one interview question is drawn from its topics or known issues.

The interviewer never reads your documents directly. It knows only the brief you approved.
