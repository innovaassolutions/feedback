title: Documents and the session brief
description: Upload a session's materials. The AI librarian reads them, drafts the brief, and answers questions from them.
---

Open a session from **Admin** to manage its **Documents** and **Session brief**.

## Uploading documents

Choose a **File**, give it a **Title** (it defaults to the file name), choose **Who can see it**, then choose **Upload document**.

- **Accepted files:** PDF, Word (DOCX), Markdown, plain text and VTT transcripts from recorded sessions, up to 25 MB.
- **Learners and agents:** learners can open and download it from the session page, and the AI uses it.
- **Agents only:** only the AI uses it, to draft the brief and answer questions. It's never listed or linked for learners or sponsors. Use this for trainer notes or a session recording's transcript.

<Callout title="On the hosted platform, keep files under 4.5 MB for now" type="warn">
  The hosting platform limits a single upload to 4.5 MB. Larger files will fail until uploads go straight to storage. Split a large PDF, or save it with smaller images.
</Callout>

After uploading, the document shows **Reading…** while the librarian extracts the text, then **Ready**. If a file can't be read, for example a scanned PDF with no text or a corrupt file, it shows **Couldn't read** with the reason. Choose **Remove** to delete a document and everything taken from it.

## The session brief

Once the documents are read, the librarian drafts the session brief:

- **Objectives**: what learners should be able to do.
- **Topics covered**: the main topics, one per line.
- **Known issues**: anything learners are likely to struggle with.

Edit it, then choose **Approve brief**. The interviewer uses only an approved brief. Until you approve it, interviews still work but don't ask about this session's particular topics. With an approved brief, one interview question is drawn from its topics or known issues.

The interviewer never reads your documents directly. It knows only the brief you approved.
