## Removing your named feedback

At the bottom of the course home page, under **Your data**, choose **Remove my named feedback and questions**, then confirm. This deletes:

- every interview you gave as **Named** in this course
- every question you posted under your name

Anonymous and private feedback can't be traced back to you, so it can't be found to be removed. It stays and keeps counting toward the course's results.

## How long things are kept

- **Conversations** are deleted a year after they were submitted (your course may set a different period). The ratings and themes stay, because they no longer say anything about one person.
- **Private interviews** keep no conversation at all. It is deleted as soon as it has been analysed.
- **Unfinished interviews** close after 24 hours without activity. Ratings you gave are kept, and the conversation isn't analysed.
- **Audio** is never stored by the platform.

See [Privacy and data](/docs/how-it-works/privacy) for the full picture.
