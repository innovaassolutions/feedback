---
title: Giving feedback
description: The feedback interview, step by step. It takes five to eight minutes.
---

After a session, your instructor opens it for feedback. Its card on the course page then shows **Feedback open**. Open the session and choose **Give feedback**, or scan the QR code your instructor shows.

You give feedback once per session. Feedback stays open for a week after the session opens (your course may set a different window). You get an email reminder a day after it opens and another a day before it closes, unless you've already given feedback.

## 1. Before we start

The first screen asks two things.

**How anonymous do you want to be?** Choose **Named**, **Anonymous** or **Private, summary only**. Anonymous is chosen to begin with. Each choice says exactly what your instructor will see. [Anonymity levels](/docs/learners/anonymity) explains them in full. You can change your choice again at the end, before you submit.

**How would you like to answer?**

- **Start with voice**: the interviewer reads each question aloud and listens to your answer. Your browser asks for the microphone. You can switch to typing at any point. See [Answering by voice](/docs/learners/voice).
- **Start with chat**: you type, or tap the answer chips.

## 2. Welcome

The interviewer welcomes you and repeats the anonymity level you chose. In chat, select **Start** when you're ready. With voice, listen to the welcome and then say that you're ready.

A progress marker at the top shows the four stages: **Consent**, **Ratings**, **Conversation** and **Summary**.

## 3. Ratings

Four quick questions, each with concrete answers:

| Question | Answers |
| --- | --- |
| Could you now do what this session taught, on your own? | Yes, without help · Yes, with the guide beside me · Only with someone helping · Not yet |
| How was the pace of the session? | Too slow · About right · Too fast |
| How much of this session will you use in your work? | Most of it · Some of it · A little · None yet |
| How likely are you to recommend this session to a colleague, from 0 to 10? | 0 to 10 |

Every question also offers **Prefer not to say**, and choosing it is never questioned. In chat, tap a chip to answer. With voice, say your answer. The interviewer reads the choices out and asks once more if it didn't understand. If a question isn't clear, say so: the interviewer explains it in a simpler way and asks again.

Your course's questions may be different: instructors can edit the [interview guide](/docs/running-a-course/interview-guide).

## 4. Conversation

A few open questions, one at a time:

- what was most useful, and why
- what was unclear or confusing
- what blocked or slowed you down, during the session or since
- what you would change for the next session
- one question about a topic from this session
- whether the changes made since the last session helped

Answer in your own words. Short answers are fine, and you can skip anything. The interviewer follows up at most once on each topic and doesn't suggest answers.

- **If you ask a question about the course material**, the interviewer offers to post it to the session's Q&A board for you. It posts the question only if you agree.
- **If something is stopping you**, such as "I still can't log in", the interviewer passes it to your instructor straight away by email. Your name is included only if you chose Named.
- **If you want to stop**, say so. The interviewer moves straight to the summary.

The interview ends after about 14 of your answers or 15 minutes, whichever comes first.

## 5. Check the summary

The interviewer sums up what it heard in three to five points. This is what your instructor will read as your main points, so check it carefully:

- Edit any point by typing over it, or remove it.
- Choose **Add a point** to add something.
- Or tell the interviewer what to change, and it rewrites the summary.
- Answer any rating question listed under **Still to answer**. Every question needs an answer, even if it's Prefer not to say.
- Change your anonymity level here if you've changed your mind.

When it's right, choose **Submit feedback**. Finally, you can tell us whether the interview was **Helpful** or **Not helpful**.

<Callout title="If you close the page">
  An unfinished interview is kept in the browser you started it in. Open the session again in that browser to carry on. After 24 hours without activity it is closed. The ratings you gave are kept, but the conversation isn't analysed.
</Callout>

## What happens next

Your instructor reviews the feedback before the next session. What they changed appears under **You said, we did** on the next session's page, and the next interview asks whether it helped.
