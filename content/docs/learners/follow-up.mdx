---
title: The follow-up interview
description: About a month after the course, a five-minute check on what you've used at work.
---

Feedback straight after a session captures what's fresh. The follow-up asks what has stuck. It opens 30 days after the course's last session (your course may set a different gap). You get an email when it opens, and a card appears on the course home page: **Has the course helped at work?** Choose **Take the follow-up**.

It works like the session interview: you choose an anonymity level, then voice or chat, answer one rating question and have a short conversation.

- **Rating:** "Since the course, have you used what you learned at work?" Yes, regularly · Yes, once or twice · Not yet · I haven't needed to · Prefer not to say
- **Conversation:** what you've used at work (an example helps), what stopped or slowed you, and what support would help you use it more.

The answers add an **Applied at work** tile to your instructor's dashboard.
