---
title: Asking questions
description: Each session has a Q&A board. An AI answers from the course's sources, and your instructor reviews the answers.
---

Open a session from the course page. Under **Questions** you can ask anything about that session's content, for example "How do I…?". Q&A stays open after feedback closes.

## Posting a question

Type your question and choose **Post question**. You can post it anonymously: an anonymous question stores no author and shows as "Anonymous learner".

If someone has already asked your question, choose **You have this question too** instead. Each learner can vote once per question, and the board lists questions by votes and then by time. New questions and answers appear without reloading the page.

## Answers

An AI answers straight away, using only this course's sources: the session's documents, the product documentation and answers the instructor has already checked. Every answer says where it found the information. Each source is marked **Session document**, **Documentation** or **Verified answer**, and you can open it unless it's a document only the instructor can see.

Each question shows its status:

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Waiting for an answer** | Just posted |
| **AI answer · not reviewed** | Answered from the course's sources. Your instructor hasn't checked it yet |
| **Sent to instructor** | The sources don't cover it, so the instructor was emailed and will answer |
| **Answered by instructor** | The instructor wrote or checked the answer |

If two sources disagree, the answer says so and the question also goes to the instructor.

## Questions during the interview

If you ask a question about the material during a feedback interview, the interviewer offers to post it to the board for you. It posts the question only if you agree, and only once. The interviewer doesn't answer it itself: it keeps to your feedback, and the librarian answers from the course's materials, with their sources. The question shows under the conversation, and the answer appears there a few seconds later while you carry on. If the materials don't cover it, it says your instructor will answer it on the board.
