---
title: Anonymity levels
description: What your instructor sees at each level, and how the platform keeps its word.
---

You choose a level before the interview starts and can change it on the summary screen before you submit. **Anonymous** is chosen to begin with.

| Level | What your instructor sees | What is stored |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Named** | Your name, your ratings and the conversation | The interview is linked to you |
| **Anonymous** | Your ratings and the conversation, with no name or time | Nothing links the interview to you |
| **Private, summary only** | Nothing individual. Your answers count toward the scores and themes | Nothing links the interview to you. The conversation is deleted once it has been analysed, and no quotes are kept |

<Callout title="In a small group, writing style can identify you" type="warn">
  Even without a name, the way you write can give you away in a small group. Private is the safest choice.
</Callout>

## How anonymity is kept

These rules are built into the platform. They don't depend on anyone, or any AI, remembering to follow them.

- **No link to you.** An anonymous or private interview has no field that points to you. The platform records that you've given feedback, so it doesn't remind you again, in a separate place with a date and no time. Nothing connects the two records.
- **Hidden until enough people respond.** Instructors see anonymous conversations only once at least five learners have submitted for that session. They see them in random order, with no names or times. Private conversations are never shown individually.
- **Results wait for a group.** The dashboard shows only the number of responses until five learners have responded.
- **The interviewer doesn't know your name** in an anonymous or private interview.
- **Voice is never recorded by the platform.** Your speech is turned into text, and only the text is kept. The voice service is told your name is "Learner" unless you chose Named.
- **Logs never contain what you said.**
- **No breakdowns by job title or team.** Results can't be cut into smaller groups.

Your course can change the group size of five. See [Settings and limits](/docs/how-it-works/settings-and-limits).

## Questions on the Q&A board

When you post a question, you can post it anonymously. An anonymous question stores no author and shows as "Anonymous learner".
