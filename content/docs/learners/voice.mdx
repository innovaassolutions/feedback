title: Answering by voice
description: Talk to the interviewer instead of typing. Voice and typing share one interview.
---

## Starting with voice

Choose **Start with voice** on the first screen. Your browser asks to use the microphone. Allow it. The interviewer reads the welcome aloud, including the anonymity level you chose. When it finishes, say that you're ready, and it asks the first question.

You can also turn voice on later with the **Speak** button next to the message box. While voice is on, the button shows **Listening**. Select it again to stop.

## Tips

- **Speak naturally and take your time.** The interviewer waits for you to finish, including short pauses while you find a word.
- **Answer the rating questions in your own words.** "About right" or "eight" works. If the interviewer isn't sure what you meant, it asks once more.
- **Ask for a question again** if you didn't follow it. The interviewer explains it more simply.
- The interviewer speaks slowly and uses plain English, for people who speak English as a second language.
- What you say appears in the chat as a message with a microphone mark, so you can see what was heard.
- **Switch to typing at any time.** Tap a chip or type a message, and the interview carries on where it was.

## If voice doesn't work

If the microphone is blocked, or the voice service can't be reached, a notice appears and the interview carries on as a chat. To allow the microphone later, change the site's permission in your browser settings and choose **Speak** again.

## What is kept

Only the text of what you said is kept, just like a typed answer. The platform never stores audio. The voice service receives the name "Learner" for anonymous and private interviews.
