The Course Feedback Platform collects feedback after each session of a training course. A short spoken or typed conversation with an AI interviewer replaces the usual survey form. Instructors see the results on a dashboard before the next session, change what needs changing, and learners see those changes.

## How a session's feedback works

1. **The session is delivered.** The instructor moves it to **Feedback open** and shows the QR code on the last slide.
2. **Learners give feedback.** Each learner has a five-to-eight-minute interview. It asks four quick rating questions, then a short conversation about what was useful, what was unclear and what got in the way. At the end the learner checks a summary of their main points and submits it.
3. **The answers are analysed.** An AI analyst groups what each learner said into a fixed set of themes and picks out blockers, suggestions and quotes. Every number on the dashboard is counted by the platform from saved answers. The AI never makes up a number.
4. **The instructor reads the results** on the dashboard, alongside the transcripts learners agreed to share.
5. **The loop closes.** The instructor records what they changed ("You said, we did"). Learners see it on the next session's page, and the next interview asks whether the change helped.

Between sessions, learners can ask questions about the material on each session's Q&A board. An AI answers straight away from the course's own documents and says where it found the answer. An instructor reviews the answers.

## Who does what

| Role | Sees and does |
| --- | --- |
| **Learner** | Sees the course's sessions and materials, gives feedback, asks questions and votes on them |
| **Instructor** | Runs sessions, uploads documents, invites learners, edits the interview guide, reads results and transcripts, answers questions |
| **Sponsor** | Sees the aggregated results and the changes made. Never sees transcripts or quotes |
| **Organization admin** | Manages the organization's branding and courses |
| **Platform admin** | Creates organizations and watches the quality of the AI agents |

One person can hold more than one role. For example, an instructor can also be enrolled as a learner.
