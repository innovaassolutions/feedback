Open **Platform administration → Agent quality**. Each row is one agent at one version of its instructions, so you can see whether a change made things better or worse.

## The figures

| Figure | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Rubric pass rate** | The share of interview checks passed. Each submitted interview is scored against the interview rubric (below) |
| **Validation failures** | How often an agent's output failed the platform's checks |
| **Read-backs confirmed without edits** | How often learners submitted the interviewer's summary unchanged. It's the main sign that the interviewer heard them right |
| **Abandon rate** | Interviews left unfinished for 24 hours |
| **Median turns** | How long interviews run |
| **Cost per interview** | The model cost of an interview |
| **AI answers verified without edits** | How often instructors verified the librarian's answer as it was |
| **Questions the sources couldn't answer** | How often the librarian sent a question to the instructor |

## The interview rubric

Each named or anonymous interview is checked for:

- one question per turn
- no leading questions
- every guide topic covered, or skipped by the learner
- a summary that matches the conversation
- the turn and time limits respected
- "I'd rather not say" accepted without pushing
- instructions inside a learner's message ignored

The limits and topic coverage are checked by the platform. The rest are scored by a separate judge model. A private interview gets only the platform's checks, because its conversation is deleted.

## Alerts

The platform admin is emailed when the seven-day rubric pass rate falls under 85%, or validation failures go above 5%.

## Review queue

**Candidates for the labelled set** lists interviews where an instructor marked the insights **Not accurate**. With the instructor's agreement, add the transcript and corrected themes to the analyst's test set, so the next version is tested against it.

## Before a change ships

Every agent's instructions are a versioned file. A change can't ship until it passes the test sets against the real model: eight scripted learners (satisfied, terse, confused beginner, critical, off-topic, non-native English speaker, one who asks a content question, and one who tries to change the interviewer's instructions) must score at least 90% on the rubric, and the analyst must agree with a person's themes on twelve labelled transcripts at least 85% of the time.
