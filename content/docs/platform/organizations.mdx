Platform admins see **Platform administration** on their courses page, or go to `/platform`.

## Creating an organization

Under **New organization**, enter:

- **Organization name**: as it should appear, such as "Streamspace".
- **Web address**: a short name for the organization's pages on this platform, such as `streamspace` for `feedback.innovaas.co/streamspace`. It isn't the organization's own website: enter `streamspace`, not `www.streamspace.ai`. It fills in from the name, uses 3 to 50 lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens, and can't be changed later, because links, emails and QR codes use it. A few words the platform uses itself, such as `docs`, `admin` and `platform`, are reserved.
- **Logo**: PNG or SVG, up to 2 MB.

Choose **Create organization**. The platform stores the logo, asks the brand designer for a theme, and opens the organization's **Branding** page so you can check and approve it. See [Branding](/docs/running-a-course/branding).

Then create its first course under **Courses**, and add its admins, instructors and sponsors under **People**. See [People](/docs/running-a-course/people).

The **Organizations** list links to each organization's **Branding**, **Courses** and **People** pages.

## Separate by design

Each organization's data is kept apart by the database itself. Every record carries its organization, and the database refuses to return a record from another organization, whatever the page asks for. A learner in one organization can't see that another exists. An address from another organization shows "Page not found", exactly like an address that doesn't exist.
