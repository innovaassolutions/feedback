description: There are no passwords. You sign in with a link sent to your email.

## Signing in

1. Open the platform and choose **Sign in**. If you came from your organization's own address, such as `/streamspace`, the sign-in page shows its logo.
2. Enter the email address you were invited with and choose **Email me a sign-in link**.
3. Open the email and select the link. **Open it on the same device and browser**, because that's where you'll stay signed in.

The link works once and expires after an hour. If it has expired or was already used, the page says so. Ask for a new one.

<Callout title="No email?" type="warn">
  The page says "If your address is registered, a sign-in link is on its way" whether or not the address has an account. That way nobody can use the page to find out who is enrolled. If no email arrives within a few minutes, check your spam folder, then check with your instructor that you were invited with that exact address. The platform never creates an account at sign-in: someone has to invite you first.
</Callout>

If you ask for links too quickly, the page asks you to wait a minute and try again.

## Joining from a QR code or link

Instructors show a QR code at the end of a session. Scanning it opens that session's feedback interview. If you aren't signed in yet, you're asked to sign in first and then taken straight to the interview.

## After signing in

- With one course, you go straight to its home page.
- With several courses, or as a platform admin, you see **Your courses** and choose one.
- If you aren't part of any course yet, the page says so. Your instructor sends the invitation.

The header of each course page shows the organization's logo, the pages you can use (Sessions, Dashboard, Course setup), your email address and **Sign out**.
