---
title: Settings and limits
description: Each course has its own limits. These are the defaults.
---

These limits are settings on each course, not fixed in the platform. To change one, ask the platform team.

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Minimum group size | 5 | Results and anonymous transcripts stay hidden until this many learners have responded to a session. It can go down to 3 for a small cohort, at a cost to anonymity |
| Turn limit | 14 | After this many learner answers, the interviewer moves to the summary |
| Time limit | 15 minutes | After this long, the interviewer moves to the summary |
| Feedback window | 7 days | How long a session stays **Feedback open** before it closes by itself |
| Follow-up delay | 30 days | How long after the course's last session the follow-up interview opens |
| Retention | 365 days | How long conversations are kept after submission |

Other fixed limits:

- **Documents:** PDF, DOCX, Markdown, plain text and VTT, up to 25 MB (4.5 MB on the hosted platform for now).
- **Logos:** PNG or SVG, up to 2 MB.
- **Documentation sites:** up to 500 pages, re-read weekly.
- **Unfinished interviews** close after 24 hours without activity.
- **Net Promoter Score** shows only at ten or more answers.
- **Language:** English.
