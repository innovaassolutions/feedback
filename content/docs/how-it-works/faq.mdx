## For learners

**Why an interview instead of a form?**
A form gets ticks. A short conversation gets the reason behind them: what was unclear, what got in the way, and what would help. That's what an instructor can act on.

**Can my instructor tell it was me?**
Only if you choose **Named**. Anonymous and private interviews store no link to you, and anonymous ones are shown only once five or more people have responded, in random order, with no time. In a small group your writing style might still give you away, so choose **Private** if that worries you.

**I made a mistake in my answers. Can I fix it?**
Before submitting, yes: edit the summary, or tell the interviewer. After submitting, the feedback is final. If you gave it as Named, you can remove it from **Your data** on the course page.

**Can I give feedback twice?**
No. One interview per session, plus the follow-up at the end of the course.

**The interviewer didn't understand me.**
Say so, or say it another way. In voice, you can switch to typing at any point. If a rating question isn't clear, say "I don't understand", and it explains the question more simply.

## For instructors

**Why don't I see any results?**
Results appear once five learners have responded to the session. Before that, only the count shows.

**Why is one learner missing from the transcripts?**
Private interviews are never shown individually, and anonymous ones appear only from five responses. All of them still count in the scores and themes.

**Can I change the questions?**
Yes, under **Course setup → Interview guide**. Keep the ability, pace and recommend items measuring the same thing, because the dashboard tiles read them.

**The AI gave a wrong answer on the Q&A board.**
Choose **Edit answer** or **Replace with my answer**. Your answer becomes a source for later questions, so the same mistake isn't repeated.

**How do I add another instructor, or a manager who wants to see the results?**
An organization admin adds them under **Settings → People**: an instructor to run the course, or a sponsor to view its results. See [People](/docs/running-a-course/people).

## For admins

**Can each organization have its own web address?**
Not yet. Each organization has a path, such as `/streamspace`. Custom domains may come later.

**Which languages are supported?**
English for now.
