---
title: You said, we did
description: Record what you changed because of feedback. Learners see it, and the next interview asks if it helped.
---

Feedback is worth giving when people can see it acted on. At the bottom of a session's results, record each change:

- **You said**: what learners raised, such as "The Core Agent part felt rushed".
- **We did**: what you changed, such as "Session 2 starts with a ten-minute Core Agent recap".
- **Status**: **Planned**, **In progress** or **Done**.

Choose **Record change**.

## Where it shows up

- **Learners** see the list under **You said, we did** on the next session's page.
- **The interviewer** knows about the change in the next session's interviews and asks whether it helped. It asks without describing the change, so the answer isn't led.
- **Sponsors** see the list on the dashboard.
- **The printed report** includes it.
