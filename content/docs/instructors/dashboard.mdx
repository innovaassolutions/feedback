Choose **Dashboard** in the header. It shows the course as a whole: the trend across sessions, and an **Applied at work** tile once follow-up interviews come in. Under **Sessions**, open a session to see its results.

## A session's results

### Nothing shows until five learners respond

Until a session has five submitted interviews, the page shows only the count and "Results appear once 5 learners have responded". This protects small groups: with three responses, a score or quote could point to one person. A course can change the number. See [Settings and limits](/docs/how-it-works/settings-and-limits).

### Tiles

| Tile | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Responses** | Submitted interviews against learners enrolled, such as 7 of 8 |
| **Able to apply** | Learners who chose either "Yes" answer to "Could you now do what this session taught, on your own?" |
| **Pace about right** | Learners who chose "About right" for pace |
| **Recommend** | The average from 0 to 10, with the spread |
| **Net Promoter Score** | Appears only at ten or more answers. Below that it swings too much to mean anything |
| **Q&A** | Questions open against answered, the median time to an answer, and how many went to the instructor |

Tiles show counts as well as percentages, because cohorts are small. Percentages are out of all responses, so a "Prefer not to say" counts in the total but not toward any answer. The Recommend average leaves it out.

### Charts

- **Could you now do it on your own?** and **Pace**: how the answers spread.
- **Mentions by theme**: positive and negative mentions for each theme.
- **Across sessions**: Able to apply, Pace about right and Recommend for each session.
- **Questions: open and answered**.

Every chart has **Show table**, which shows the same numbers as a table, for screen readers or for copying.

The themes are a fixed list, so they compare across sessions and courses: Content and relevance, Pace and timing, Instructor delivery, Hands-on practice, Materials and documentation, Platform and access, Logistics, and Support and follow-up.

### Summary, blockers, suggestions and quotes

- **Summary**: a short paragraph written by the AI analyst. The platform checks every number in it against the real figures. If the summary contains a number the platform didn't give it, a plain summary written from the numbers is shown instead, with a note saying so.
- **Top blockers**: what stopped or slowed learners.
- **Suggestions**: what learners would change.
- **In their words**: short quotes. A quote is kept only if it appears word for word in what the learner said. Private interviews never give quotes.

### Acting on it

Below the results, record what you changed in **You said, we did**. See [You said, we did](/docs/instructors/you-said-we-did). Use **Transcripts** to read the conversations, and **Print report** for a printable version.

<Callout title="Every number is counted, not written">
  The ratings come straight from the answers learners saved. The AI writes the summary and sorts comments into themes, but it never produces or changes a number.
</Callout>
