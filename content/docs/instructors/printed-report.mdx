---
title: The printed report
description: A printable session report for meetings and records.
---

From a session's results, choose **Print report**. The report page has no navigation and is laid out for A4. Choose **Print**, or use your browser's print command. Most browsers can save it as a PDF.

The report has the organization's logo and shows:

- the tiles: responses, able to apply, pace, recommend
- the ability and pace spreads
- the themes, with positive and negative mentions
- the summary, top blockers and suggestions
- the **You said, we did** list

The same rules apply as on screen: nothing shows until five learners have responded.
