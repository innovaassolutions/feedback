From a session's results, choose **Transcripts**. Only instructors can open this page. Sponsors can't.

## What you see

- **Named** interviews show the learner's name, the confirmed summary and the full conversation.
- **Anonymous** interviews show the confirmed summary and the conversation with no name or time. They appear only once the session has at least five submitted interviews, and in random order, so the order can't hint at who submitted when.
- **Private** interviews never appear. They count toward the scores and themes, and their conversations are deleted once analysed.

Each interview shows the **Confirmed summary**, the points the learner checked and submitted, then the **Conversation**. Spoken turns carry a microphone mark.

## Is the AI's reading right?

Under each interview, **Are these insights accurate?** asks you to check the themes, blockers and suggestions the analyst took from it.

- **Accurate**: nothing more to do.
- **Not accurate**: the interview goes on the platform admin's list of candidates for the analyst's test set, so the analyst can be improved.
- **Analyse again**: queues a fresh analysis.

An interview marked **Needs review** is one the analyst couldn't read reliably: its output failed the platform's checks twice. It still counts toward the ratings.

## Transcripts are kept for a year

Conversations are deleted a year after they were submitted (your course may set a different period). The scores and themes stay.
