title: Answering questions
description: Review the AI's answers, answer what the sources don't cover, and build up the course's knowledge.
---

Each session's page has a Q&A board. Learners' questions are answered straight away by the AI librarian from the course's sources. Your job is to check those answers and to answer what the sources can't.

## When you're emailed

You get an email, "A question needs your answer", when:

- nothing in the sources answers the question, so the question shows **Sent to instructor**, or
- two sources disagree. The AI's answer says so, and you're asked to check.

You're also emailed straight away when a learner reports a blocker during a feedback interview, such as "I still can't log in". The learner's name is included only if they chose a named interview.

## Reviewing an answer

On the session page, an AI answer shows **AI answer · not reviewed** and the sources it used. As an instructor you can:

- **Verify** it. The label changes to **Answered by instructor**.
- **Edit answer**, to correct it.
- **Replace with my answer**, to write your own.
- **Delete answer** or **Delete question**.

Learners can't answer questions themselves.

## Verified answers become sources

When you write or verify an answer, it becomes a source for later questions. A similar question next week is answered citing it as a **Verified answer**. If you edit the answer, the source changes with it. If you delete it, it stops being used.

Questions that went to you are also a record of what the materials don't cover, which is worth fixing in the next version of the guide.

## The Q&A tile

A session's results include a **Q&A** tile: open against answered questions, the median time to an answer, and how many went to the instructor.
