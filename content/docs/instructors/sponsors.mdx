---
title: For sponsors
description: What a sponsor, such as the client's manager, sees.
---

A sponsor follows a course's results without seeing anything individual.

| Sponsors see | Sponsors don't see |
| --- | --- |
| The dashboard tiles and charts | Transcripts |
| The themes and the summary | Quotes ("In their words") |
| The **You said, we did** list | Individual blockers and suggestions |
| The printed report | Anything from a single learner |

The small-group rule applies: until five learners have responded to a session, its results show only the response count.

Sign in as usual. The course header shows **Dashboard**. Open a session there to see its results.
